## Interpretation

A 1e-6 relative budget on an option price is a hundredth of a basis point on a
$250 premium. The binding constraint is the normal CDF's 7.5e-8 absolute error,
which propagates to roughly `spot * 7.5e-8`, or 1.9e-5 on a $250 underlying:
two thousandths of a basis point.

- norm_pdf(0) = 0.398942280
- quantum = 1.0e-9 (the representation's smallest step)

## Known limits this report does not cover

- Deep out-of-the-money premiums below ~1e-9 flush to zero; the two terms of
  the closed form cancel into representation noise there. Monotonicity in
  volatility is asserted only up to that noise band (see the lattice tests).
- No jump-diffusion, stochastic-volatility or barrier models: this is the
  Black-Scholes and CRR family, priced exactly, not a general model suite.
- Volatility is an input. There is no live implied-volatility surface for
  tokenised equities to read, and pretending otherwise would be the honest
  thing to call out in a review.
